# Deep Research Template

Use this structured format whenever Darin asks for deep research on a topic.

## Research Process

### 1. Identify the Primary Source
- If a specific article/link is provided, try to access it first
- Fallback to fetch via browser if paywalled/blocked
- Note if the primary source is inaccessible and why

### 2. Multi-Source Gathering (minimum 5 sources)
- **Primary news** — web_search for the specific topic, 5-10 queries
- **Conflicting perspectives** — search for critiques, counter-arguments
- **Market/business data** — Sacra, Crunchbase, company filings
- **Historical context** — how did we get here
- **Key players** — who are the main actors

### 3. Structured Analysis Sections

```
## 📌 Thesis — One-line summary of what's happening

## 🏢 Key Players — Who, what, valuation, key stats

## 📊 Data & Market Context — Revenue, growth rates, market size

## ⚠️ Risks & Challenges — Security, legal, competitive threats

## 🔮 Outlook — Where is this going

## 💎 Bottom Line — My take (with reasoning)
```

### 4. Source Attribution
- Include source URLs
- Note when data is estimated vs confirmed
- Flag sources that may be unreliable

### Sources to Always Check
- Sacra Research — for startup revenue/valuation data
- Business Insider — for insider reporting
- TechCrunch / The Information — for breaking tech news
- SEC filings — for public company data
- Company blogs — for official narratives
- Competitor analysis — for market positioning
