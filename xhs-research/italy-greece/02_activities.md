# Italy + Greece — Activities

## Rome (4 Days)

### Day 1 — Ancient Rome 🏛️
1. **Colosseum & Roman Forum** — Iconic, book tickets in advance
   - [XHS](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/explore/6927c2df000000001e0304bc)
   - Tip: "一定要按照票面的时间提前前往"
2. **Palatine Hill** — Views over Roman Forum
3. **Trastevere** — Evening stroll and dinner in charming neighborhood

### Day 2 — Vatican & City Icons
1. **Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel** — Book guided tour
2. **St. Peter's Basilica** — Free entry, dome climb for views
3. **Pantheon** — Ancient architectural marvel
4. **Trevi Fountain & Spanish Steps** — Evening walk
   - Tip: "推荐在罗马选个好位置住然后步行，到处都是景观"

### Day 3 — Borghese & Baroque
1. **Borghese Gallery** — Must book in advance, guided tour recommended
   - Tip: "找个讲解很值得"
2. **Borghese Gardens** — "罗马之行最美地之一"
3. **Piazza Navona** — Baroque fountains and atmosphere
4. **Via del Caravita / St. Ignatius Church** — 3D ceiling frescoes

### Day 4 — Culture & Departure Prep
1. **Castel Sant'Angelo** or revisit favorite spots
2. Afternoon flight to Athens

## Athens (3 Days)

### Day 5 — Ancient Athens
1. **Acropolis & Parthenon** — "一定要看日落"
   - Book ahead, go early morning for fewer crowds
   - [XHS](https://www.xiaohongshu.com/explore/6996beec000000000a02c497)
2. **Acropolis Museum** — Modern, well-designed
3. **Plaka neighborhood** — "找一家百年咖啡店坐下"
4. **Lycabettus Hill** — Sunset views over the city

### Day 6 — City Highlights
1. **Ancient Agora** — Heart of ancient Athens
2. **Temple of Olympian Zeus**
3. **Panathenaic Stadium** — First modern Olympics venue
4. **Constitution Square** — Watch changing of the guard
5. **Monastiraki Flea Market** — Souvenirs, handmade sandals

### Day 7 — Final Day & Departure
1. **National Archaeological Museum** or relax in Plaka
2. Last-minute souvenir shopping
3. Evening flight home
