## Athens — Must-Eat

### Traditional Greek 🇬🇷
1. **Plaka restaurants** — Traditional tavernas with outdoor seating
2. **Psiri neighborhood** — Trendy spot for dinner and drinks

### Local Specialties
3. **Greek coffee** — Strong, rich, served with sediment
4. **Souvlaki & Gyros** — Street food staple
5. **Moussaka** — Traditional baked dish
6. **Greek salad (Horiatiki)** — Fresh tomatoes, cucumber, feta, olives
7. **Feta & Olives** — Greek staples
8. **Baklava** — Sweet pastry dessert

### Budget Notes (from XHS traveler)
- Rome: Lunch ~€15-25/person, Dinner ~€25-40/person
- Athens: Dinner ~€20-35/person
- Street food/gelato: €5-10
