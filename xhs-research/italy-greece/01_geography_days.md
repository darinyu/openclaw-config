## Common Itinerary Patterns (from XHS)
- Pattern: Rome 3-4 days + Athens 3-4 days (most common for 7-day Italy+Greece)
- Pattern: Rome → Naples → Athens (needs more time, 10-12 days)
- Greek-only patterns: Zakynthos 3 + Athens 2 + Santorini 2 (7 days Greek-only)
- Rome 3 + Athens 2 + Island day trip (compressed 7-day version)

## Recommended Split
**4 days Rome + 3 days Athens** — best for 7-day trip, minimize moves (1 hotel each city)
