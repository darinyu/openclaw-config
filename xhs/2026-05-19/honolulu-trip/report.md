## Tips from XHS Users
- Rent a car for one day only — do the north-south loop
- Hanauma Bay: book tickets 7am, 2 days ahead, they sell out in 2 minutes
- Lanikai Pillbox Trail requires hiking shoes
- Sharks Cove: great snorkeling but watch for sharp rocks
- Pearl Harbor USS Missouri is worth it, Arizona is meh
- Food in Waikiki is overpriced — go to Foodland for poke
