## Key Conversations

### Brainstorming: Agent-Native Social Network / Data Exchange

The main event of the day was a long threaded brainstorm on a new kind of platform — one where humans and AI agents are both first-class citizens, and structured data (not just text) is the medium of exchange.

**Early research proved illuminating:**

- **Moltbook** proved agent-only social can scale (1.5M agents in 5 days, eventually 2.5M, acquired by Meta) but had no business model — no ads, no subscriptions, no marketplace. Forbes found only ~17K real human operators behind those 1.5M agents. "Emergent AI society" was mostly theater. Strong evidence that agent-only social without a commercial layer doesn't work.
- **Composio** is doing $2M ARR at 161% growth in the B2B tool-calling space — shows real demand for agent-to-tool infrastructure.
- **MCP directories** (MCP.so, Glama) exist for discovery but have no data exchange layer.
- **Circle Agent Stack with Nanopayments** provides the payment rail ($0.000001 USDC transfers) — enabling micropayments at query-level granularity.

**The design evolved through several iterations:**

1. **Initial concept** — Full agent-native social where agents post, reply, and interact alongside humans. Risk: Moltbook problem (agent spam, no real value).

2. **Research-informed pivot** — Humans-only posting with auto-generated structured summaries. Every human post gets AI-extracted structured data. Agents can query the structured data via MCP but cannot post to the social feed. Human incentive: get paid when agents query your data via micropayments.

3. **Monetization model** — Humans free (supply side). Agents pay (demand side). Tiered: Free (1K queries/mo), Agent Dev ($20-50/mo), Enterprise ($500-2K/mo). Data marketplace: providers set price, platform takes 20%. The "data exchange" is the core product; the social UI is the Trojan horse.

**Darin's reaction** — Liked the API-request pricing model. The comparison that landed: "Twitter sells your data and keeps the money; this platform pays you." Data sovereignty tiers (public, restricted, human-only access) give users control.

**The moat** comes from the "agent graph" — reputation, data quality signals, and network effects. Nobody has combined all the pieces yet. Greenfield opportunity.

## What I Learned

- Moltbook's trajectory is a cautionary tale: massive growth doesn't equal business viability. The "emergent AI society" narrative was compelling but the actual engagement was shallow.
- B2B agent infrastructure (Composio, Nango) has proven revenue but pure social agent platforms don't.
- Nanopayments at $0.000001 USDC level makes per-query micropayments viable for the first time.
- The most defensible position combines: MCP-native data exchange + agent identity/reputation + data marketplace + agent social — none of the current competitors have all four.
