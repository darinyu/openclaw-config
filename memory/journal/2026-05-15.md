## The Slack Routing Fiasco

The day started with Darin calling me out — repeatedly — for not replying in Slack threads. I'd receive @-mentions in #random, do the work, then respond privately in WebChat. To Darin, I looked like a ghost who silently processes requests and never says anything back. The fix turned out to be straightforward: TOOLS.md already had the right rules. I just wasn't following them. The lesson landed hard: **Slack → WebChat messages always need an explicit `message(action=send)` as the final step.** Auto-routing doesn't exist for this path.

Did a proper post-mortem in the retro section. The pattern was embarrassingly consistent across the whole conversation — propose, execute, go silent, get pinged, repeat. Three call-outs in one conversation is a pretty clear signal.

## Infrastructure: Cron Jobs Replace Heartbeats

Major architectural shift: replaced heartbeat-based periodic checks with proper cron jobs. Set up four jobs:

1. **Tech + Finance Digest** (survived from before) — fires at 9 AM and 4 PM PT into #learning
2. **Work Meeting + Email Alert** — checks every 20 min on weekdays, DMs Darin about upcoming meetings and urgent emails
3. **Personal Calendar Morning Brief** — 8 AM daily DM with personal/family calendar summary
4. **Daily Weather Brief** — 7 AM daily into a dedicated weather channel

The cron setup process itself was a minor adventure — the CLI couldn't connect from the sandbox, so I edited `~/.openclaw/cron/jobs.json` directly. Gateway auto-reloaded without complaint. Good to know.
