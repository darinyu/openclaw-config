*Generated: May 20, 2026 | Depth: Deep | Sources: 30+ | Confidence: High*

## Executive Summary

OpenAI has experienced the fastest revenue growth in tech history, scaling from $1B in 2023 to $20B+ in 2025 — roughly 10x growth in two years. By February 2026, annualized revenue surpassed $25B, driven by 50M+ paid ChatGPT subscribers, enterprise adoption reaching 40%+ of revenue mix, and early advertising experiments. However, the company is hemorrhaging cash: projected losses of $14B in 2026, $27B cash burn, and no profitability expected until 2029-2030. Massive infrastructure commitments — $600B in compute spend through 2030 — and escalating inference costs ($8.4B in 2025, projected $14.1B in 2026) are straining the business model. A $122B funding round in March 2026 valued OpenAI at $852B, with IPO plans targeting mid-to-late 2027 at up to $1T valuation. Advertising is emerging as a critical revenue pillar, with internal targets of $2.5B in 2026 ad revenue, rising to $100B by 2030.

### Microsoft Partnership Renegotiation

The Microsoft-OpenAI relationship has been restructured significantly:

- **Revenue share**: OpenAI pays Microsoft 20% of revenue, **capped at $38B total** through 2030 (vs prior trajectory of ~$135B — saving ~$97B)
- **Leaked docs**: OpenAI paid Microsoft $865.8M in revenue share during first 3 quarters of 2025
- **Azure exclusivity ended**: OpenAI can now offer products on any cloud provider
- **Microsoft IP license**: Non-exclusive, extends through 2032
- **Microsoft retains resell rights**: Through 2032
- **Microsoft developing in-house models**: Investing in rivals (Meta, Inflection AI) — relationship is deteriorating
